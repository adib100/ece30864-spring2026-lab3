(a) : Agile development is helpful for its high adaptability and its speed, but lacks in its overall predictability and is more resource intensive. 
Meanwhile, non-agile development focuses on a clearer structure than agile, and is more predictable, but fails to be flexible in its project path, as it follows a more linear path than agile.

(b) : The Scrum Master handles the day-to-day activities, such as stand-ups, and generally acts as a coach to the team. 
The product manager works on the backlog, and priorities the features and mitigates issues between the stakeholders and the team.

(c): Three types of meetings include:

    - Daily stand-ups
        Consists of synchronization between team members and any important project updates.

    - Sprint planning
        Held at the start of a sprint, discusses role management and adds items to backlog

    - Sprint retrospective
        Held at end of sprint, reflection on what went right/wrong, and discussion on improvements for next sprint
